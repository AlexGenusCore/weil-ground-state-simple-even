I recommend **not yet releasing this version as a completed computer-assisted proof**. I read all 848 lines of `main.tex` (release candidate), examined the bibliography, code and records, and ran read-only checks. The central argument is plausible: the parity, monotonicity, deflation and Schur-complement reasoning withstand inspection, and all shipped interval comparisons pass. I found no fatal counterexample to the main theorem. However, essential derivations for Piece IV are excluded from the release, two printed upper bounds lack a demonstrated outward-rounding guarantee, and the invocation of Connes–van Suijlekom omits a substantive hypothesis. These require correction before I would endorse the advertised certification. The paper generally distinguishes finite-window results from RH appropriately, and its disclosure of computational failures is candid.

**Issues**

1. **Major — §7.3, Theorem 7.3 and its proof; source lines 551–575, 801. Essential certificate derivations are unavailable in the release.**  
   The theorem’s abstract Schur argument is sound conditional on the stated bounds. But the paper supplies neither the diagonal-entry formula nor the formulas and proofs establishing the computed \(\tau_N\), \(r_M\), generators and assembly debit. Saying that \(\tau_N\) “bounds” the relevant operator does not establish that the program’s number does so. These are central mathematical obligations, not implementation details. I located and inspected the private `PROOFS.md` in the adjacent working tree; its existence does not make the public release complete.  
   **Fix:** publish the specification and derivations, especially P14–P22, as a versioned supplement, with an explicit mapping between formulas, code and recorded fields.

2. **Major for the numerical by-products — §8, Table 8 and Remark 8.2; lines 710–719. Outward rounding is not established for two displayed bounds.**  
   The original even-sector records at \(L=2\) and \(2.25\) give identical 15-digit strings for `lam0_mid` and `lam0_upper`. Printing an upper endpoint with `.str(15, radius=False)` does not guarantee rounding upward: I reproduced a case where this operation returns a decimal strictly below its input. A tiny ball radius does not determine the direction of decimal rounding. Consequently, the asserted rounding slack in Remark 8.2 is not established by these records.  
   **Fix:** use a directed decimal exporter, or explicitly enclose the serialization error and round more coarsely. At \(L=2\), the shipped `upper_rerun_L2.jsonl` already supplies the safer value \(1.84681871820109\times10^{-283}\). Replace the \(L=2.25\) value with an outward-enclosed value and ship its evidence. This finding does **not** invalidate the main interval proof: \(U(2)\) enters no step, and \(2.25\) lies outside its range.

3. **Major for the claimed application — abstract and §1.2; lines 53, 94, 829. Connes–van Suijlekom’s precise hypothesis is omitted.**  
   Their Theorem 6.1 requires essential self-adjointness of the operator defined on trigonometric polynomials. A self-adjoint operator obtained by closing a form on \(C_c^\infty\) does not automatically establish that hypothesis. The source itself distinguishes its informal introduction from the precise theorem. ([Connes–van Suijlekom, Theorem 6.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.23257#S6))  
   **Fix:** state the full hypothesis and prove the required core/realisation identification, or establish an appropriate form-core version before claiming its consequence here. This is separate from the simple-even theorem for the paper’s own Friedrichs realisation.

4. **Minor, but a useful simplification — Remark 4.2, §§7.4–7.5 and §11(2); lines 208, 523, 631–637, 824. Rule E is mathematically unnecessary with the shipped records.**  
   Monotonicity gives
   \[
   \lambda_2^{\mathrm{ev}}(1.92364501953125)
   \ge \lambda_2^{\mathrm{ev}}(1.9326171875)
   \ge 1.3019115357395\times10^{-236}.
   \]
   This exceeds the odd lower bound at the failed window, approximately \(1.23237044193436\times10^{-236}\). Substituting that later-window certificate preserves every step. I ran the checker with
   `--l2-from 1.92364501953125=1.9326171875`; all 53 comparisons pass.  
   **Fix:** retain the honest history of the registered failure, but present this monotonicity substitution as an alternative proof avoiding rule E. The statement that rejecting rule E necessarily reduces the mathematical range is too strong.

5. **Minor — Proposition 5.13(a), proof; line 372. Algebraic sign error.**  
   From the definition,
   \[
   \Phi=H-P+m^2(P-\bar\mu)
        =H-\bar\mu-(1-m^2)(P-\bar\mu),
   \]
   whereas the displayed proof writes \(P+\bar\mu\) in the last factor.  
   **Fix:** replace that plus sign by a minus sign. The subsequent conservative estimate using \(A+\bar\mu\) remains valid, so this does not require changing the reduction.

6. **Minor — §§1.1 and 2.3; lines 65–71, 139. The initial domain is overstated.**  
   Compact support and \(L^2\) membership alone do not guarantee a finite logarithmically weighted Fourier integral. The later form-domain discussion recognises this, but the opening definition does not. Also, the discreteness paragraph would benefit from explicitly establishing closability before invoking the associated operator.  
   **Fix:** define the form first on smooth compact tests, then its closure; alternatively declare an extended-valued form on \(L^2\). Explain closability through the closed Fourier-multiplier form plus bounded prime and pole perturbations.

7. **Minor — §8, Galerkin construction and Proposition 8.1; lines 690–697. The stated measure has a singular density.**  
   The archimedean weight
   \[
   k_L(r)=\frac{2Le^{-Lr/2}}{1-e^{-2Lr}}
   \sim \frac1r
   \]
   is not a smooth integrable density at zero. The code correctly uses cancellation, but the prose describes ordinary moments of a smooth measure.  
   **Fix:** define the regularised moments explicitly, including
   \[
   \int_0^2 k_L(r)\bigl(P_j(-1)-P_j(r-1)\bigr)\,dr,
   \]
   and explain the removable singularity and separate diagonal correction. Include the Chebyshev rounding bound used by the implementation.

8. **Minor — §1.3, §1.4 and §8; lines 104, 114, 117, 716. A relevant earlier rStation result is missing.**  
   Paper 83, published **31 October 2026 at 19:33 UTC**, claims unconditional absolute sampling bounds at the \(4\pi e^{2L}\) scale and corresponding upper bounds for every fixed-index eigenvalue in both sectors. This updates the discussion that attributes that scale’s rigorous upper-bound treatment only to conditional Paper 81. It supplies neither simplicity nor lower bounds, and its non-explicit constants do not establish a better explicit value at \(L=2.25\). ([Paper 83](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/83))  
   **Fix:** add a version-specific comparison while retaining the distinction between asymptotic upper bounds and the present lower certificates.

9. **Minor — prior-work survey and novelty qualification; lines 96–117. A directly relevant competing public claim is absent.**  
   Brendan Siche’s repository claims spectral simplicity through Krein–Rutman arguments. Its README contains serious apparent defects: a finite sum of compressed translations is generally not compact, and the even pole operator in this paper’s normalisation has norm
   \[
   2\int_{-L}^{L}\cosh^2(x/2)\,dx=2(L+\sinh L),
   \]
   contradicting the asserted logarithmic growth in the multiplicative window parameter. I therefore do not regard the repository as established prior proof. Nevertheless, it is directly relevant to a survey of public claims. ([Repository](https://github.com/Architect-Resonance/Riemann-Hypothesis))  
   **Fix:** establish its first-publication date and briefly distinguish the claim, without granting it theorem status.

10. **Minor — §9, especially line 746. Numerical extrapolation becomes an assertion about a limit.**  
    Four resolutions with decreasing jumps do not establish that the continuum eigenfunction is continuous, has the proposed logarithmically softened singularity, or that the jumps tend to zero. The section’s opening disclaimer is appropriate, but the later categorical wording exceeds the evidence.  
    **Fix:** write “consistent with” and “appear to tend to zero”; define the effective resolution parameter and provide the fitting procedure and raw measurements.

11. **Minor — §10.1; line 754. The shipped-run count and total cost are stale.**  
    There are **24** Piece III `g_*.json` records, not 19. Their recorded seconds multiplied by worker counts total approximately **2006.37 worker-process hours**, not 1177. The 16-record main-theorem subset does reproduce approximately **814.00 hours**.  
    **Fix:** regenerate these totals from the records.

    - **Lemma 5.10, line 314:** “every \(k\)” should be restricted to \(1\le k\le N\), or \(\lambda_k(A)\) must be defined beyond the finite head.
    - **§5.2, line 265:** the elementary envelope application should explicitly require \(T^\sharp\ge3/2\); all actual cutoffs satisfy it.
    - **§2.2, line 136:** the leading singular cancellation is \((g(0)-g(x))/x\), not \((g(0)-g(x))/(2x)\). The domination argument survives.
    - **§5.2, line 334:** define the rescaled function explicitly in the formula for \(g(Ls)\); the current notation reuses \(f\) with incompatible coordinates.
    - **§7.5, line 631:** replace “every hard gate being true” by “every other hard gate being true”; V4 is itself listed as a hard gate.
    - **Lemma 5.4’s attribution:** Zhu’s cited Lemma 3.1 states the envelope for \(t\ge15/4\). The present proof improves the threshold to \(3/2\); identify it as an improved version. ([Zhu, §3](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.24827v2#S3))

**Claims independently verified, and how**

- **Parity splitting, the simplicity criterion, support monotonicity and interval gluing:** checked the form identities, zero-extension argument and min–max inequalities, including prime-power activation endpoints.
- **Rank-one deflation and Corollary 5.15:** checked the Courant–Fischer argument and its combination with the head/tail block estimate. No eigenvector-accuracy assumption is needed.
- **Elementary analytic estimates:** checked the Binet argument, digamma monotonicity, Bessel bounds and backward-ratio enclosure. For Lemma 5.1, independently recovered \(\int_{\mathbb R}|\varphi''|=6/u^2\) and hence the constant \(0.8125\).
- **Gauss–Legendre constant:** checked Trefethen’s actual Theorem 19.3. The paper’s corrected \(n\)-point exponent \(\rho^{-2n+2}\) is the appropriate reindexing. ([Original text, equation 19.8](https://people.math.ethz.ch/~hiptmair/Seminars/RAP_22/TRE13.pdf#page=153))
- **Piece I computation:** reproduced the Arb enclosure calculation,
  \[
  I_{\rm bound}\approx0.5939785226511983,\quad
  C_{\rm ev}\approx4.876361676705376,\quad
  C_{\rm odd}\approx0.761665418948984.
  \]
  Using the paper’s rounded constants and shipped endpoint records gives gaps exceeding \(0.41727509\) and \(0.66242480\), validating the quoted \(0.4172\) and \(0.6624\).
- **Shipped arithmetic:** ran `check_theorem.sh` successfully using the available python-flint environment. Piece III–IV checksum checks passed. Regenerated `generated.tex` in memory; it matched byte for byte. These verify record consistency and comparisons, not their entire computational generation.
- **Independent Galerkin assembly:** constructed autocorrelation polynomials with exact rational arithmetic and integrated the position-side formula using mpmath. Forty entries across both parities at \(L=0.05,0.8,1.5,2\) agreed with the upper-bound implementation to at most \(6.5\times10^{-62}\).
- **Independent trigonometric assembly:** used exponential-product convolution and separate high-precision position-side integration. Eighteen entries at \(L=1.4035,1.92364501953125,2\), across both sectors, agreed within \(1.4\times10^{-55}\).
- **Green-response identity and Lemma SR:** checked their algebra. Sixty small random tests of the Python SR implementation, with generator dimensions 4 and 8, satisfied its claimed residual lower bound. These do not validate the production C runs.
- **Reported failure and repair:** the three failed records have V4 false at index 37375 with \(N=37888\); the rule-E record passes at \(N=38912\). The independent monotonicity substitution described above also passes.
- **Normalisation:** matched the pole, prime and archimedean terms against CCM’s equations (3.7)–(3.11) under \(u=e^x\); their \(2\theta'(t)\) equals this paper’s \(H(t)\). This resolves the form-level comparison more explicitly than the current affine-rescaling caveat. ([CCM, §3](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.22755#S3))
- **Literature statements:** confirmed Suzuki’s Theorem 1.4, Zhu’s Theorem 6.2 and retraction discussion, CCM’s Theorem 3.6, and the positivity-only scope of rStation Papers 45 and 60. The latter papers are correctly credited as methodological predecessors. ([Suzuki](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.09096), [Zhu](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.24827v2), [Paper 45](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/45/v1), [Paper 60](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/60))
- **Explicit comparison at \(L=2.25\):** recomputed the logarithms of the stated Mori bound, theta bound and numerical even bound: approximately \(-1052.91975\), \(-504.00296\) and \(-1093.82973\), respectively.

- I could not validate historical source revisions that were not recorded, or recover a directed-rounding certificate from the original \(L=2.25\) output.
- The rStation JSON API endpoints failed through the available network tools. I inspected accessible paper pages and both public listing pages instead. Zenodo records also repeatedly failed to load; therefore several deposited manuscripts, exact theorem numbers and priority timestamps remain unverified.
- I did not verify the full proofs of all cited unrefereed claims, including Kramarenko-Byrd’s claimed interval, or establish exhaustive worldwide priority.
- I did not reproduce the singularity measurements or infer continuum regularity from them.

The main result remains credible enough to merit further review, but this review does **not** certify its complete computer-assisted proof.
